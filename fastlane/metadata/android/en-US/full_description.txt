Baresip Pro is a modern, privacy-first SIP (Session Initiation Protocol) client designed specifically for lightweight, high-performance voice communications on Android. It pairs the standard-compliant Baresip C audio engine with a fluid Jetpack Compose (Material 3) user interface.

Features:
• Crystal-Clear HD Audio: Wideband and fullband codecs including Opus, AMR-WB, AMR-NB, G.722, G.722.1, G.729, iLBC, Codec2, and standard G.711 (PCMA/PCMU).
• Privacy & Security: End-to-end media encryption with ZRTP and SRTP (SDES and DTLS-SRTP), plus secure SIP signaling over TLS and WSS. Zero trackers, no telemetry.
• Modern Material 3 Design: One-handed bottom navigation bar, ergonomic keypad dialer, dynamic color support, and automatic dark/light theme switching.
• Integrated Communications: Multiple SIP accounts with live registration indicators, local call recording, contact management, and SIP instant messaging.
• Native Performance: Powered by native C libraries (libre, libbaresip, OpenSSL) for low latency, fast startup, and minimal battery consumption.
