Baresip Pro Max is a modern, privacy-first SIP (Session Initiation Protocol) client designed for high-definition voice and video communications on Android. It pairs the battle-tested Baresip C/C++ engine with a fluid Jetpack Compose (Material 3) user interface.

Features:
• HD Voice & Video: High-definition video streaming powered by Camera2 and hardware-accelerated AV1, H.265 (HEVC), H.264, VP8, and VP9 codecs. Wideband and fullband audio with Opus, AMR-WB, G.722, Codec2, and standard PCMA/PCMU.
• Privacy & Security: End-to-end media encryption via ZRTP and SRTP (SDES and DTLS-SRTP), plus secure SIP signaling over TLS and WSS. Zero trackers, no third-party telemetry.
• Modern Material 3 Design: One-handed bottom navigation bar, ergonomic keypad dialer, dynamic color support, and automatic dark/light theme switching.
• Integrated Communications: Multiple SIP accounts with live registration indicators, local call recording, contact management, and SIP instant messaging.
• Native Performance: Powered by native C libraries (libre, libbaresip, OpenSSL) for low latency, fast startup, and minimal battery consumption.
